## Clean (the probe could have failed and did not)

- **The guarded writes on production's own triggers** (rolled back, as the E2E Enterprise owner through RLS). These replay the filters `SupabaseMemberRepository.updateFields` builds:
  - a payment with a stale due-date guard: **0 rows**; with the guard it read: **1**; the same guard again (the double submit): **0**;
  - the expiry cron on an active member, guarded on status and due date: **1**; a suspend guarded on the status read before it: **0**;
  - a plan switch guarded on `monthly` / `5000` (the integer a JS number serialises to) against a stored `5000.00`: **1**; a stale plan guard: **0**;
  - effects across the block: `member_count` 17 → 17 (one in, one out), **one** status-history row for the member paid, and **one** access-sync job, for the due-date change but not for the plan change (the enqueue trigger compares status, due date, start date and `verified_at` only);
  - into active at a Lite gym's cap (the gym row altered inside the block): **`MEMBER_LIMIT_REACHED`**, nothing written; at 99, **1 row** and the count reached 100.
  - Re-queried after: the gym is still `enterprise` with `member_count` 17 = 17 active, and no history row or job is newer than before.
- **The triggers ignore a same-value status write.** `LIFECYCLE_FIELDS` writes `status` even when it stays `active`. `record_member_status_change` (`IS DISTINCT FROM`), `sync_gym_member_count` and `enforce_active_member_quota` (`OLD <> NEW`), read from `pg_get_functiondef`, all compare old and new.
- **No guard column can be null.** `status`, `next_due_date`, `plan_type` and `plan_amount` are `NOT NULL`, and production has **0 nulls** in 726 members, so `.eq(column, null)` cannot arise. `plan_amount` is `numeric(10,2)`, and **no** production value has a fractional part.
- **Production's PostgREST parses the plan guard as supabase-js sends it.**
  - `plan_amount=eq.1000000` (annual) and `eq.1` (daily), the extremes in production, match, as do `eq.5000` and `eq.5000.00`.
  - `eq.5000.001` and `eq.4999.99` match nothing.
- **Custom plan types: nothing to probe.** `plan_types` holds 25 rows across 8 gyms, all built-in values, and members carry only the six built-ins. The custom-plan claim rests on the local check in `member-writes-inverted-check.md` alone, because production has no custom plan to test it on.
- **The cross-site guard on the four security routes, in production.** Anonymous `text/plain` POSTs with `Sec-Fetch-Site: cross-site` to phone, username, email and password: **403** with "This request must come from Metric itself", not a Vercel challenge page.
- **The discovery test catches what it is for.**
  - A new unguarded `POST` route dropped into `settings/security`: the test fails.
  - The guard call removed from the phone route: the test fails.
  - The route was restored byte for byte, and the mutant was deleted.
- **One conflict message.** Among member writes, `ConflictError` is thrown only by the repository's guard, and the payment, day-pass, suspend, activate and cancel routes all answer `ResponseBuilder.conflict(error.message)`. `toApiRequestError` falls back to the caller's sentence for any body `response.json()` cannot parse, such as an HTML 504, a plain-text Vercel error or a challenge page.
- **The docs' guard table matches the code.** The three status crons pass `expectedStatus` and `expectedNextDueDate` and count a conflict as `skippedChanged` in the summary and the response. Suspend, cancel and reactivate pass `expectedStatus`. A payment and a day pass pass `expectedNextDueDate`, plus `expectedPlan` on a switch.

## What this says about the method

- **A claim copied between four docs looks like four confirmations.** R2 was written once and repeated in the workflow, the route, the ops doc and the master reference, and the handoff reasoned from it. Each copy read as corroboration; one `grep` of the call sites contradicted all four.
- **A guard is only as wide as the folder its test reads.** The discovery test is sound for `settings/security` (both mutants fail it), and that soundness is what made the missing login guard invisible. A green test about a scope says nothing about what lies outside it.
- **A re-baseline records whatever the data is that day.** The size of the PNGs (909 KB to 54 KB) said more than the pixel tolerance ever could.
- **A screenshot can picture a state the DOM never holds.** The analytics trend was cut off at August in the image while every path and clip rectangle in the page was whole. A `fullPage` capture resizes the viewport, and the charts re-ran their reveal inside the shot. Reading the DOM is what told the instrument apart from the screen.
- **No failure folder is not a pass.** The first mutant run failed two screens, restarted its worker into the sign-in limit, and never ran four others. I first read two of those four as passing. The run summary's counts, not the artefacts, say what ran.
