# Outreach series: inverted check

A falsifying check of the four-email outreach series, focused on unsubscribing and on the automated sends (2026-10-06, after the 82-business list was imported and before any email reached it). Every finding was reproduced or measured first, fixed test first, then mutated back to the defect to show the test catches it (M1–M6, plus O8's test). The design is in `marketing-email.md`, "Outreach".

## The automations: inverted check (2026-10-07)

A falsifying check of the webhook, the safety brake and the summary (`marketing-email.md`, "Looking after itself"), before merge. Real emails went to a throwaway campaign: the owner's Gmail under three `+` aliases, Resend's test addresses (`bounced@`, `complained@` ×2, `suppressed@`, `delivered@resend.dev`) and `mailto-test@go.metricgymos.com`. One-click, the /unsubscribe form and an email to `unsubscribe@` were made against the live endpoints. Resend's webhook still pointed at production, where the route was not yet deployed. Routing it to a temporary tunnel was refused, so each event's **exact payload was read from Resend's log** and replayed, signed with the webhook's secret, to the branch running locally against the production database. The campaign, its 9 Resend contacts and every row were deleted afterwards.

**Checked, and holding:**

- **Bounce:** `bounce.type` is `"Permanent"`, and `from` is the outreach address. The address became `bounced`, with Resend's message as the detail.
- **Complaints:** both complaint addresses became `complained`. The second **paused the campaign**, and "Outreach paused: 2 spam complaints" reached hello@ ("delivered"). It was sent once.
- **Suppressed:** `bounced`, recorded as a `suppressed` event, so the brake does not count it.
- **One-click** (Gmail's button) and the **footer form**: each produced a `contact.topics.updated`, and each address became `unsubscribed`.
- **Unsubscribe by email:** a message to `unsubscribe@go.metricgymos.com` arrived as `email.received` (`to` and `received_for` hold the bare address), and the sender became `unsubscribed`.
- **Forward:** mail to any other `go` address reached hello@ as text, with Reply-To set to the writer.
- **Headers as delivered:** the `List-Unsubscribe` mailto is `unsubscribe@go.metricgymos.com`.
- **Repeats:** every event replayed a second time changed nothing. There were no new events, and the forward kept the same Resend id (idempotency key).
- **Pause and resume:** while paused, a run sent nothing. After `--resume` the brake counted from zero.
- **Locally, against the branch:** the cron answered `notStarted` for the live campaign, and 401 without the bearer. Forged and unsigned webhook posts got 401 over the public internet.

**Known and accepted:**

- **Gmail does not report spam complaints per message.** Gmail gives senders only an aggregate rate, in Postmaster Tools, and only at volume. 35 of the 82 addresses are Gmail, and more are on Google-hosted domains. For them, the brake's complaint count stays at zero. Gmail's "Report spam & unsubscribe" still sends the one-click, so the address is stopped, but it counts as an unsubscribe. An unsubscribe-rate threshold could stand in for complaints; this is not built, and is the owner's decision (HANDOFF §6).
- **Resend's own signature on a production delivery** was not observed, because the route was not deployed. The replay used the same secret and the Standard Webhooks scheme the SDK verifies. After deploy, the first real event shows 200 in Resend → Webhooks.

## Checked, and holding (live, on production)

- **The footer link.** A prospect's /unsubscribe page answers 200, shows the Marketing choice and the address, and is `noindex`. **Opening it changes nothing**: the address read as still subscribed afterwards.
- **One-click from a mail app.** The exact RFC 8058 request answered 200, and Resend read Marketing `opt_out`. A forged token got 400. A GET (a person opening the URL) is sent to /unsubscribe with 303.
- **After an unsubscribe** the next week's run skipped the address and marked it `unsubscribed`, while the others got email 2.
- **Resend's log** carries `kind=outreach`, `campaign` and `email` = 1 to 4 on every send.
- **The list:** 82 rows, 82 distinct addresses, all `active` at step 0, each with its website as the source. 82 Resend contacts were added, each with a token, in no segment.
- **No address can get the same email twice:** the step is claimed in the database, Resend gets an idempotency key per address and step, and the six-day gap applies.

## Known and accepted

- **A person's reply asking to be removed** arrives in the hello@ inbox and still needs `npx tsx scripts/send-outreach.ts --remove their@email`. Mail apps that unsubscribe by email no longer do: since 2026-10-07 the header's `mailto` is `unsubscribe@go.metricgymos.com`, which the Resend webhook acts on (`marketing-email.md`, "Looking after itself").
- ~~Spam complaints and hard bounces are not recorded in the ledger.~~ Since 2026-10-07 the webhook ends the series for them (`bounced`, `complained`), and the safety brake pauses the campaign when they spike.
- **Opening /unsubscribe shows two checkboxes,** including "Product and security updates", which a prospect never receives. It is harmless, since Marketing comes pre-ticked from the link.
- **`--run` by hand does not wait for the start date.** It is a deliberate manual override.
