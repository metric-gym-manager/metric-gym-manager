1. Introduction
Welcome to Metric Gym OS - a gym management platform built for fitness businesses in Sri Lanka and beyond. We are committed to protecting your personal data and handling it responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your data when you use our platform, including our web dashboard, mobile applications, and any related integrations.

This policy applies to:
• Gym owners, administrators, and staff who use the platform to manage their fitness business
• Gym members whose personal information is processed through the platform by gym operators

2. Information We Collect
We collect and process the following categories of personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022:

Information We Collect from Gym Members:
• Identity Data: full name, gender, date of birth (age calculated automatically), selfie
• Contact Data: phone number, emergency contact details
• Biometric Data: fingerprint or face scan (where applicable), used to enable secure check-in/check-out and to open the gym's doors
• Membership Data: membership plan details, start and expiry dates, membership status
• Attendance Data: check-in/check-out logs, attendance history

How biometric data is handled: Biometric data is special category personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, and we treat it accordingly.
• A fingerprint is only ever recorded with the member's explicit consent, given at the moment it is recorded rather than buried in a registration form, and we keep a record of when that consent was given
• The fingerprint template is created on the reader itself and is held by the gym's access-control hardware provider. We never receive, process or store a fingerprint image or template - what we hold is a numeric reference to the member's record on that hardware and a note of whether a fingerprint has been registered
• Members may withdraw consent at any time by asking their gym to remove their fingerprint. Doing so deletes the template from the reader and clears the consent record, and the member will be asked again before any new fingerprint is recorded
• Providing a fingerprint is never a condition of membership. Members who decline continue to use the gym by whatever other means it offers

Important notice: We do not require or recommend the collection of National Identity Card (NIC) numbers or home addresses for gym members. This data has no operational requirement on our platform, and collecting it may violate the data-minimization requirements of the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 - we discourage gym operators from collecting it and gym members from providing it.

Information We Collect from Gym Operators:
• Identity Data: full name of the account creator (admin), gym/business name
• Contact Data: email address, phone number, gym address, gym website (optional)
• Staff Data: staff names and roles (where assigned)

Financial Data:
• Membership fee amount, payment status, payment dates, renewal dates
• Platform subscription status, renewal dates, billing history, transaction records
(Payment card details are handled exclusively by our payment gateway partners and are never stored on our servers.)

Operational Data:
• Member attendance records and check-in/check-out logs
• Membership plan details and expiry information
• SMS reminder logs and communication history

Technical and Usage Data:
• IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type
• Login timestamps, session data, access logs
• Dashboard interactions, feature usage patterns, revenue and retention analytics

Cookies and Website Analytics:
We use Google Analytics (provided by Google LLC) on our website and web application to understand how visitors and operators use Metric Gym OS. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies on your device and may collect:
• Pages and screens viewed, referring sites, and approximate location derived from IP address
• Browser, device, and operating-system information
• A client identifier stored in cookies so repeat visits can be distinguished from new ones

This processing is based on our legitimate interest in measuring and improving the service. We do not use Google Analytics to identify gym members or to sell advertising against your data. Google may process this information on servers outside Sri Lanka, including in the United States. Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can refuse or delete these cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Blocking analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information
We use your personal data only when the law allows us to, in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022. The purposes of processing, and their legal basis, are:
• Provide and operate the platform and its features, authenticate users, and manage role-based access for gym staff - necessary for the performance of our contract with you
• Manage member registrations, memberships, and renewals - necessary for the performance of our contract with the gym operator
• Tell a gym's door readers how long each member's membership entitles them to enter, and record the entries and refused attempts those readers report back - based on the member's explicit consent to biometric processing, which they may withdraw at any time
• Send automated payment reminders and renewal notifications via SMS - based on the consent obtained from the member at registration
• Generate attendance reports, revenue dashboards, and retention analytics for gym owners, and conduct demographic analysis using age and gender data where provided - our legitimate interest in helping gym operators run their business
• Provide customer support and respond to inquiries - necessary for the performance of our contract with you
• Measure visits to our website and how the platform is used, using Google Analytics - our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the service
• Monitor platform performance, detect errors, and ensure system reliability - our legitimate interest in keeping the platform secure and available
• Detect, prevent, and address fraudulent or unauthorized activity - our legitimate interest in preventing fraud
• Comply with legal obligations applicable to our operations - necessary for compliance with a legal obligation

4. SMS Communications
Metric Gym OS sends SMS notifications to gym members on behalf of gym operators. These messages are limited to:
• Payment due reminders
• Membership renewal notifications
• Expiry alerts

By registering with a gym that uses Metric Gym OS, members consent to receive these essential SMS notifications as part of the platform's core functionality. Every SMS we send includes information on how to stop receiving it, free of charge, and a member may withdraw consent at any time by contacting their gym directly, which will action the request at no cost.

5. Data Security
We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:
• SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
• Encrypted storage for sensitive data at rest
• Role-based access control (RBAC) to restrict data access to authorized personnel only
• Secure cloud infrastructure with high availability
• Regular security reviews and access audits

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to promptly addressing any incidents that may occur.

6. Data Retention
We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

Retention periods:
• Member data: retained for the duration of the membership and for a reasonable period afterward to maintain business records
• Payment records: retained for a minimum of 7 years for accounting and tax purposes
• Statistical data (payment patterns, attendance trends, demographic information, revenue data, member-count trends, and feature usage): retained indefinitely in aggregated, anonymized form that does not identify any individual data subject
• Google Analytics records: retained by Google for the period configured in our Google Analytics property, after which event-level data is deleted or aggregated
• Door access records: entries and refused attempts recorded by a gym's biometric readers are deleted automatically 12 months after they occur
• Fingerprint templates: held on the gym's reader and by its access-control hardware provider for as long as the member is enrolled, and deleted from the reader when the member withdraws consent, is removed by the gym, or their membership ends

Data deletion:
• Member photos, fingerprints, and face scans are permanently removed upon account deletion or member removal
• Member names and contact information are securely deleted when no longer required for business purposes

Gym operators may request deletion of their account and associated member data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will securely delete or anonymize the data in accordance with applicable law.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We may share data with the following categories of service providers, each contractually required to protect your data and to use it only for the purposes we direct:
• SMS gateway providers, to deliver notifications on behalf of gym operators
• Cloud infrastructure and hosting providers, to host and process data on our behalf
• Access-control hardware providers, where a gym operates biometric door readers - limited to the member's name, a numeric reference and the dates their membership permits entry. The fingerprint template itself is created on the reader and held by that provider; it does not pass through us
• Payment processors, to process subscription and membership payments securely
• Google LLC (Google Analytics), to measure website and application usage as described in Section 2. This may include IP address, device and browser data, and pages viewed
• Professional advisers, such as lawyers or auditors, where required by law

Some of these providers may process personal data outside Sri Lanka. Where this occurs, we rely on contractual safeguards with the provider - such as data processing agreements with equivalent protection standards - consistent with Section 26 of the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

8. Your Legal Rights
Under the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to Access (Section 13) - request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and confirmation of whether it has been processed.

Right to Withdraw Consent and Object to Processing (Section 14) - withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent, and request that we refrain from further processing where processing is based on our legitimate interests. You may object to Google Analytics by blocking or deleting its cookies in your browser, or by using Google's opt-out add-on.

Right to Rectification or Completion (Section 15) - request that we rectify or complete personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Erasure (Section 16) - request erasure of your personal data where processing is carried out in contravention of the Act, where you withdraw the consent processing is based on, or where erasure is required by any written law or court order.

Right to Request Human Intervention in Automated Decision-Making (Section 18) - request a review of a decision based solely on automated processing that has created, or is likely to create, an irreversible and continuous impact on your rights and freedoms.

Important notes on your rights:
• Requests must be made in writing to us using the contact details in Section 10
• We will respond to all legitimate requests within 21 working days, as required by the Act
• We may refuse a request on grounds such as national security, public order, an ongoing legal investigation, the rights of others, technical infeasibility, an inability to verify your identity, or a legal requirement to process the data
• Where a fee applies, we will tell you the amount and the reason for it
• These rights may be exercised by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of a minor, or by an heir within ten years of a data subject's demise

Lodging a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka if you believe your rights under the Act have been violated.
