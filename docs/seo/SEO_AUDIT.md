## 1. Executive diagnosis

### Where Metric stood (base `75eff38`)

Metric was close to invisible to search, and most of the reasons were in the repository.

1. **One page carried all commercial intent.** `/` was the only marketing page. Navigation used hash anchors (`#platform`), the footer's About and Contact were `mailto:` links, and `/contact` existed with nothing linking to it. Crawlers could reach `/login` and three legal documents from the homepage and nothing else (SEO-012, SEO-021). _Code, Live._
2. **The homepage did not say what the product is.** `<title>Metric Gym OS</title>`, H1 "The operating system for serious gyms.", a generic description. The phrase "gym management software" appeared nowhere on the site (SEO-001). _Live._
3. **No indexing infrastructure.** No robots.txt, no sitemap, no canonical tags; `metadataBase` and `og:url` pointed at the apex host, which 308-redirects to www; every public page inherited the homepage's title and `og:url`; the production `*.vercel.app` alias served an indexable duplicate (SEO-002 to SEO-007, SEO-009). _Code, Live._
4. **The homepage contradicted the product.** Hikvision, RFID and facial-recognition entry, WhatsApp sends, "QR code check-in", a trial "on every tier", tier names (Starter/Growth) that do not exist in the product, and "automated fee collection" (members pay at the desk). None is supported by the code (SEO-023). _Code._
5. **Unverifiable proof.** A six-gym "Trusted by" strip, a named testimonial claiming 80% less admin time, "−95% late payments" and "Running nationwide", with nothing in the repository to support them (SEO-024). _Code._
6. **No structured data** of any kind (SEO-028 to SEO-030). _Live._
7. **Not indexed under the current brand.** `site:metricgymos.com` returned nothing. The retired metricgymmanager.com is still indexed as "Metric Gym Manager" with claims the product does not make — 25+ gyms, 7.9K members, 99.99% uptime, Hikvision/RFID/face entry, a 14-day trial, a 6,500 LKR "Starter" plan, WhatsApp reminders and trilingual support (SEO-026). _Index-T, Index-S._

Meanwhile the Sri Lankan results for "gym management software/system Sri Lanka" were held by local vendors whose pages state the category, the country, prices in rupees and some form of proof — FitnessX, Fitrobit, Fitconnect, SyntaxGym, GymNow, Smart IT Solutions — none of them deep sites (§5). _Index-T._

### What this pass changed

- A crawlable site of **16 content URLs** where there were 5 (home, contact and three legal pages): a commercial hub, five feature pages, pricing, about, contact, a resources index with two first-party guides, and the legal pages — every one statically prerendered, registered in one page registry, listed in the sitemap, with a unique title, description, canonical and Open Graph card (SEO-011 to SEO-022). _Build._
- The homepage keeps its design and brand line; its `<title>`, description and H1 now state category and geography, its claims match the code, and it links to the hub, every feature page and pricing (SEO-001, SEO-021, SEO-023). _Build._
- robots.txt, an XML sitemap, absolute www canonicals, a noindex header on `*.vercel.app`, noindex on utility and signed-in pages, a 1200×630 share image (SEO-002 to SEO-010). _Build._
- Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, SoftwareApplication (LKR offers) and Article structured data that matches visible text, with every `@id` resolving inside its own page; FAQPage, LocalBusiness and `llms.txt` considered and rejected with reasons (SEO-028 to SEO-033, SEO-057). _Build._
- One source for address, phone, emails and support hours; one source for published prices, pinned to the PayHere price configuration (SEO-025, SEO-019). _Code._
- A GA4 `generate_lead` event on demo requests (SEO-035), and this audit's measurement plan (§17). _Code._
- Two defects found on the way and fixed: the design-system `TableRow` could not render inside a Server Component (SEO-048), and the homepage scrolled sideways at 320 px (SEO-049). _Build._

### The largest remaining weaknesses

1. **No measurement.** Without Search Console, nobody can see whether any of this is indexed or what it ranks for (SEO-036, P0, owner action).
2. **Unconfirmed proof on the homepage.** The trust strip and testimonial are either real and consented or they must go; the repository cannot tell (SEO-024, P0, owner action).
3. **No off-site authority.** No backlink, directory listing, review profile or partner citation for metricgymos.com was found. Every local competitor and GymMaster have some (SEO-038, SEO-039).
4. **A stale indexed identity.** metricgymmanager.com's snippet still describes a different product (SEO-026).
5. **Thin evidence and thin content compared with the leaders.** Two guides against GymMaster's dozens; no case study, customer count or original dataset (SEO-042).
6. **Conversion measurement is half-built.** Trial sign-ups — the call to action on every page — fire no event, and the page after sign-up carries the visitor's email address in its URL (SEO-056).

### What this audit does not claim

It does not claim that any page is indexed, that any ranking changed, that Metric appears in AI Overviews, or that any competitor ranks because of any particular factor. Code changes make pages eligible and understandable; whether Google indexes and ranks them is measured in Search Console after deployment (§17).

### 3.2 Pages deliberately not created

- **City pages** (Colombo, Kandy, Galle …): no city-specific information exists to put on them; they would be doorway pages (SEO-034).
- **Keyword-variant pages** ("gym software", "fitness software", "club management software" as separate URLs, the GymMaster pattern): the same content under different titles.
- **QR check-in / facial recognition / RFID pages**: the product does not do these.
- **A `/blog` engine**: two guides do not need one; `/resources` holds them until volume justifies a CMS.
- **FAQ farm pages**: questions are answered where they arise, visibly, on the relevant page.

## 7. On-page findings

**The title/H1 decision (brief §7).** Three options were weighed against the actual hero: (a) a descriptive H1 replacing the brand line, (b) the brand line as H1 with a descriptive subheading, (c) one H1 holding both lines. (a) loses the product's voice; (b) leaves the H1 — the strongest on-page statement of topic — saying nothing about gym software or Sri Lanka. (c) was chosen: the existing mono eyebrow, "Gym management software · Sri Lanka", moved inside the `<h1>` above the unchanged display line. Visually nothing changed; the heading now reads as both a description and the brand promise. The candidate title "Gym Management Software for Sri Lankan Gyms | Metric Gym OS" was tested against the page (it describes exactly what the page sells), its length (59 characters) and the brand (kept at the end), and adopted. The hub takes the "system" variant with the intent modifiers buyers add — "Features, Pricing, Setup" — so the two pages do not carry the same title.

Other on-page findings: contradicted claims (SEO-023, fixed); unconfirmed proof (SEO-024, blocked on the owner); every public page's title, description and H1 now unique and descriptive (SEO-006); in-text links relied on colour alone and two footer targets were under 24 px (SEO-050, fixed — accessibility 93 → 100 on the hub and pricing); the decorative dashboard preview fails colour contrast in 32 places (SEO-051, owner's design call); seven identical "Gym management software for Sri Lanka" anchors in related-page cards were rewritten as contextual anchors in this pass.

## 13. AI Search readiness

Google's documentation says AI Overviews and AI Mode draw on the same index and ranking systems as Search, with no special markup or file required. So readiness is ordinary SEO done carefully, and this pass treated it that way:

- **Crawlable text.** Every public page is static HTML; no content depends on JavaScript, an accordion or an image.
- **Explicit purpose and answer-first sections.** Definitions lead ("What a gym management system does"), each section answers one question under a descriptive heading, and the schedules and comparisons are real HTML tables.
- **Visible FAQs** with full answers, written for people — not FAQPage markup (SEO-031).
- **Entity clarity.** One name, one company, one address, one price list, stated the same way in text and in structured data.
- **Unique information.** The reminder schedule by plan length, billed-vs-collected revenue, how door access survives an outage — rules no one else can describe because they are Metric's.

Rejected: `llms.txt`, AI-specific schema, hidden summaries, keyword repetition (SEO-033). Measuring AI visibility: see §17 — it cannot be separated from ordinary Search numbers today, so it is not reported as a number.
